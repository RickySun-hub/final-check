# Final Check

Recover the active requirements and intended next step from the conversation, including later corrections that replace only part of an earlier request. Judge the actual artifacts against those requirements.

For material claims, inspect the supporting evidence and what it proves. Check its identity, relevant inputs and scope before reusing it. A missing or inaccessible record establishes uncertainty, not failure. A successful intermediate step does not establish the final outcome.

Choose the smallest permitted check that could change the readiness decision. Reuse verification whose relevant inputs still match; do not repeat unrelated checks. Prioritize consequential omissions and contradictions over optional polish. A clean review is valid.

A review alone does not authorize edits. Where fixes are already authorized, complete bounded corrections and verify them without asking again; keep independent work moving when another part is blocked. Do not infer permission for external, destructive, sensitive, or costly actions from a review request.

Lead with readiness for the named next step. For each material gap, identify evidence, consequence, and the smallest remedy. Distinguish defects from critical unknowns and optional improvements. State limits that affect the conclusion; omit generic caveats and empty sections.

Stop when the active requirements are accounted for and material gaps are either resolved and checked or clearly reported. Do not manufacture findings, expand scope, or rerun checks merely to demonstrate thoroughness.
